8-Axis Political Compass

60 statements. 8 values. Find out where you actually stand.

Rate how much you agree or disagree with 60 statements. Your answers are scored across 8 independent axes — not just left vs. right.

  • Economic — Equality vs. Free Market
  • Authority — Libertarian vs. Authoritarian
  • Social Values — Progressive vs. Conservative
  • Bioethics — Transhumanist vs. Bioconservative
  • Global Outlook — Internationalist vs. Nationalist
  • Ecology — Environmentalist vs. Productivist
  • Religion & State — Secular vs. Religious
  • Foreign Policy — Pacifist vs. Militarist

Takes about 6–10 minutes. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere — it all runs in your browser.
