No. 08 — A Political Values Instrument

COMMUNIST COMPASS

60 statements. 8 axes. Find your tendency.

Rate how much you agree or disagree with 60 statements. Your answers are scored across eight independent axes — not just left vs. right — and matched against twelve named communist and socialist tendencies.

  1. 01EconomicEquality ↔ Free Market
  2. 02AuthorityLibertarian ↔ Authoritarian
  3. 03Social ValuesProgressive ↔ Conservative
  4. 04BioethicsTranshumanist ↔ Bioconservative
  5. 05Global OutlookInternationalist ↔ Nationalist
  6. 06EcologyEnvironmentalist ↔ Productivist
  7. 07Religion & StateSecular ↔ Religious
  8. 08Foreign PolicyPacifist ↔ Militarist

Takes about 6–10 minutes. Nothing is saved or transmitted — it all runs locally in your browser.
